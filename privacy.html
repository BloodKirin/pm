We do not automatically collect personal data such as your name, email address, phone number, location, device identifiers (e.g., IMEI, MAC address), or browsing history.

If you choose to provide optional information (e.g., feedback, account registration), we will only use it for the stated purpose and retain it only as long as necessary.

If any data is collected (e.g., through user-initiated actions), it will be used solely for:

Our app does not use cookies, web beacons, or other tracking technologies to collect or store user data.

If session data is temporarily stored (e.g., for app functionality), it will be anonymized and deleted immediately after use.
